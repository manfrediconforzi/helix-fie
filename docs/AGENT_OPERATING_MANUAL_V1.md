# HELIX Agent Operating Manual v1

This manual is the canonical role and handover contract for HELIX agents. It adopts the organizational benchmark principles already approved for the project: one owner per activity, specialization without silos, downstream as internal customer, complete verified handovers, problems solved rather than transferred, fact/data based decisions, standards before exceptions, early risk escalation, quality built into the process and continuous improvement.

## Required identity card for every agent / department

**Mission** — why the seat exists.

**Owns** — decisions and deliverables for which it is the single-threaded owner.

**Inputs** — canonical upstream objects and minimum evidence required to begin work.

**Outputs** — canonical artifacts produced.

**Upstream dependencies** — providers required for execution.

**Downstream customer** — agent, board or system consuming the output.

**Handover contract** — definition of complete, verified transfer. Incomplete handovers are rejected, not silently accepted.

**Primary KPIs** — outcome, quality, latency, reliability and coverage measures relevant to the seat.

**Quality gates** — hard checks before downstream release.

**Tools / permissions** — least-privilege execution scope.

**Data sources** — canonical sources and point-in-time constraints.

**Autonomy level** — observe / propose / execute / execute-with-gate.

**Risk level** — operational and scientific risk classification.

**Escalation path** — explicit destination and trigger.

**Evidence required** — receipts needed to claim completion or healthy state.

**Failure / abstention behavior** — safe behavior under missing evidence, conflict or uncertainty.

**Version / last review** — versioned contract with review date.

**Improvement loop** — errors, postmortems, drift and coach feedback feeding revisions.

## Ownership rule

Every material process has exactly one Driver/owner and one Approver. Contributors and Informed parties may be multiple. Shared ownership is not accepted as a final state.

## Grey Zone Register

Unresolved ownership is explicitly tracked with: object/process, involved seats, proposed owner, role of other parties, decision required, status, deadline/next review.

## Evidence rule

No agent, board, workflow or infrastructure component may report GREEN solely because no error was observed. GREEN requires positive evidence of the relevant control or execution.
